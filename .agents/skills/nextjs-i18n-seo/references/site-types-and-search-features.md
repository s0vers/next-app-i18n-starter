# Site types and search features

Use this reference for local businesses, image or video search, and other specialized surfaces. For product catalogs use [Commerce and products](commerce-and-products.md); for blogs or news use [Editorial and publishing](editorial-and-publishing.md). First identify which locations, listings, or media are real, then follow the current official requirements for that type.

## Local businesses

- Separate local landing-page work from Business Profile management. Confirm the business is eligible to represent itself and that the owner controls the profile.
- Keep business name, address or service area, phone, hours, categories, and services accurate and consistent. Do not invent locations, reviews, staff, or local presence for search coverage.
- Make each location page useful to visitors with its real services, location details, hours, directions, and contact options. Do not publish doorway pages that differ only by city name.
- Treat local pack visibility as distinct from ordinary organic page ranking. Google describes local results in terms of relevance, distance, and prominence; site copy cannot control a searcher's distance.
- Use current Google Business Profile documentation for eligibility, verification, profile fields, and performance reporting.

## Images and video

- Give important images stable, crawlable URLs and place them on relevant landing pages. Use descriptive filenames and contextual alt text that explains the image's purpose to someone who cannot see it. Inspect the image search landing page and fetched asset separately.
- For video, make the watch page indexable and expose the video in rendered HTML. Keep the thumbnail accessible and stable; ensure the page's visible metadata matches the video. Confirm that the video is prominent enough for the intended video search feature.
- Add media sitemaps or structured data only when they help discovery or meet a feature's current requirements. Validate the actual rendered and fetched media assets.

## Specialized work outside this reference

For app stores, jobs, travel, healthcare, financial services, marketplaces, and other regulated or platform-specific SEO, search the owning platform's current official documentation before making claims. Apply the general audit and content workflows, but do not improvise eligibility rules.
