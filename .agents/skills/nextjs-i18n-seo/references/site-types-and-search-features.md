# Site types and search features

Use this reference only when the site has a business or content model that needs specialized search surfaces. First identify which pages, products, listings, or media are real. Then follow the current official requirements for that type.

## Local businesses

- Separate local landing-page work from Business Profile management. Confirm the business is eligible to represent itself and that the owner controls the profile.
- Keep business name, address or service area, phone, hours, categories, and services accurate and consistent. Do not invent locations, reviews, staff, or local presence for search coverage.
- Make each location page useful to visitors with its real services, location details, hours, directions, and contact options. Do not publish doorway pages that differ only by city name.
- Treat local pack visibility as distinct from ordinary organic page ranking. Google describes local results in terms of relevance, distance, and prominence; site copy cannot control a searcher's distance.
- Use current Google Business Profile documentation for eligibility, verification, profile fields, and performance reporting.

## Ecommerce and products

- Start with product and category pages that work for shoppers. Ensure important product details, price, availability, variants, shipping, and returns are accurate and visible.
- Choose product snippets or merchant listing markup based on whether customers can purchase directly on that page. Validate each product page and compare structured data with the live offer.
- Assess Google Merchant Center feeds when the requested surface or catalog size makes them relevant. Structured data and feeds can complement one another; neither guarantees placement.
- Check variant URLs, filters, sort parameters, pagination, out-of-stock states, duplicate products, and category links. Keep canonicalization and crawl controls aligned with the intended indexable catalog.
- Check current documentation before implementing product schema, merchant feeds, reviews, or regional inventory behavior.

## Publishers and time-sensitive content

- Keep article pages accessible with clear titles, authorship, publication information, and substantive reporting.
- Add a news sitemap only for eligible recent articles and maintain it according to current publisher guidance. Do not add it to a general marketing site as a ranking shortcut.
- Preserve update dates that reflect meaningful editorial changes. Do not change dates to imply freshness.

## Images and video

- Give important images stable, crawlable URLs and place them on relevant landing pages. Use descriptive filenames and contextual alt text that explains the image to someone who cannot see it.
- For video, make the watch page indexable and expose the video in rendered HTML. Keep the thumbnail accessible and stable; ensure the page's visible metadata matches the video.
- Add media sitemaps or structured data only when they help discovery or meet a feature's current requirements. Validate the actual rendered and fetched media assets.

## Specialized work outside this reference

For app stores, jobs, travel, healthcare, financial services, marketplaces, and other regulated or platform-specific SEO, search the owning platform's current official documentation before making claims. Apply the general audit and content workflows, but do not improvise eligibility rules.
